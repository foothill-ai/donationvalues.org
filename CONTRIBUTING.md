# Contributing

Start with a GitHub issue describing your proposed source, correction, or improvement.

For a proposed source, provide its public URL, publisher, guide title, edition/date if available, and any published licensing or permission information. Public availability alone does not establish permission for CC BY 4.0 redistribution.

Do not submit copied source guides or extracted values until a maintainer has confirmed the applicable rights. Do not post private correspondence or personal contact details; permission evidence must be reviewed and summarized for public attribution without exposing private information.

For corrections, identify the affected source and item, explain the discrepancy, and link to supporting evidence. Preserve source-specific values and assumptions rather than silently averaging or replacing them with another guide's numbers.

By submitting your own original dataset contributions for inclusion, you agree to make them available under CC BY 4.0. Identify any third-party material separately; you cannot license rights you do not hold.
